# Playtest notes — 2026-07-27 (Miguel, solo session)

Source: live session over Discord chat while Miguel played the self-hosted build in
Chrome, solo, mostly bow/mage (one earlier melee run with the mace, and a long trident
run). This was the first play against the new **gold economy**, the **flagstone floor**,
the **reward-every-room** loot, and **randomized floor-1 layouts**.

Raw feedback sorted into buckets, as with [playtest-2026-07-20.md](playtest-2026-07-20.md).
Where a report was **already fixed by a commit landed the same day**, it's marked
✅ **FIXED** with the commit — a lot of this session's bugs were caught and patched
between the session and this writeup.

Overall read: he's having fun ("inclined to say it's actually fun," best moment was
buffs stacking), the new background and gold landed well, and — as last time — the
run-enders were all bugs (chest softlock, walk-out-of-room, wall-firing), not the core
loop. The dominant *design* signal is **too much of everything**: too many weapons, too
many shops, buffs too strong. The game is generous; it needs to become scarce and
choiceful.

## 2. Quick wins

Small, high-value, mostly already-scoped.

- **Weapon comparison on pickup/buy** — green/red stat numbers with up/down arrows vs. the
  currently equipped weapon. His most-wanted; I wanted it too ("keeps bothering me I can't
  tell which is better"). ✅ **DONE** — `cc04358`, merged `400afc9`.
- **Buff descriptions on pickup** — show what a buff does when you grab it, not just the
  name (it's already in the `i` inventory screen). ✅ **DONE** — `2eb8b9b`, merged `cfc462c`.
- **FPS meter** — needed to even diagnose C5. Not yet built. High priority next.
- **A button / prompt to descend to the next floor** — right now you step on stairs and go.
  He skipped most of a floor by walking onto the exit without realizing what it was. Make
  descending a deliberate interact, not a step-on.
- **Warn / confirm before descending with the floor uncleared** — an "are you sure." Both of
  us flagged that unconditional confirms get annoying in other games, so gate it on *floor
  not cleared* only, and keep it a single keypress to dismiss.
- **Full heal every floor — reconsider** — floors currently restore full HP on descent. He
  likes *some* healing but not full-every-floor. Idea floated: a one-time heal station in
  each shop, and remove the automatic full heal once other healing exists. (D-heal.)

## 3. Design signals

The through-line of the whole session: **the game is too generous.** Scarcity and
meaningful choice are the next design pass.

### 3a. Weapon flood (the loudest signal)
He ended runs holding **20 weapons** with **300–400 unspent gold**, most from floor 1,
having bought nothing. Every-room rewards + frequent shops + no inventory cap = weapons
are noise, and gold has nothing to buy.

Decisions reached in-session:
- **Weapons should be rare.** Target ~**1, maybe 2 weapon-swap options per floor** — possibly
  **per party, not per player**. ✅ **Partially landed** — `65e40c6` (merged `bedf4b3`) makes
  shops rarer and removes weapons from room-clear rewards. Re-test the density after this.
- **Shops are too dense.** He repeatedly hit 2–3 of his first 4 rooms being shops. Target
  ~**1 weapon/shop room per floor, randomized location.** I'm fine with the *first* room
  sometimes being a shop you come back to — just not the current density. (Also addressed by
  `65e40c6`; verify.)
- **Inventory cap.** Strong lean toward **1 weapon** (I want hard choices, not options);
  Miguel likes a ranged+melee pair so leans **2**. **OPEN — pick a number.** Leaning 1 or 2.
- **Higher-floor weapons should roll stronger** (not strictly better — higher chance of good
  rolls). Agreed direction, not built.
- **Give gold something to do besides weapons** — buyable buffs (I want this), heal stations,
  and the new armor/trinket slots below. Gold is currently a dead currency past ~floor 1.

### 3b. Buffs are too strong
Stacked **40% + 40% + 20% attack** (two Berserk + Ferocity) in a row. Berserk's −15% HP is
the only brake. Likely fix: **shrink the magnitudes** (e.g. 40%→~a few %) and/or lower buff
frequency — but he and I both *like* a buff every room, so favor **smaller numbers over
rarer buffs**. Vitality/HP buffs felt fairly costed by comparison.

### 3c. Armor / trinket slots (new system idea)
To dilute weapon density with *other* rewards and give gold a sink. Simple equipment slots:
**Armor / Helmet / Necklace / Ring**. Purely mechanical for now — I have no armor art, but
nothing stops stats from being worn invisibly. Miguel is drafting a list of
buffs/trinkets/armor to seed it. Open sub-questions we surfaced:
- **Which effects are player-permanent vs. weapon-bound vs. temporary?** Emerging model:
  *potions* → player; *vial of poison* → applied to the held weapon, non-transferable, maybe
  **times out** unless your class has the effect natively. I like forcing you to buff a single
  weapon; it *feels* better to keep buffs — unresolved tension.
- **Status effects as weapon buffs:** poison, slow, confusion, frozen, petrified. Frozen =
  more damage from blunt, thaws faster if hit by fire; petrified = frozen longer but takes
  less damage (stone). This is further down the pipe — noted, not scoped.

### 3d. Melee is unrewarding / range is too strong
Recurring theme. He got to floor-deep runs on bow and mage; I "usually die in the first
couple rooms with a melee weapon." Range feels inherently stronger.
- **Enemy combat is the root cause** — all enemy damage is currently *touch-based*, no telegraphed
  swings, so ranged just kites everything. This is the real fix and it's big.
- **Melee needs to be protected/incentivized** — either faster swings or *really* worth it.
- **Slow weapons feel bad** (his mace run): slow attack + hard-to-time combo. He dislikes slow
  attacks generally. Long-swing weapons have a combo-timing problem specifically.
- **Spears/trident are too strong** — great range *and* power ("probably stronger than the bow").
  Ideas: combos that start short and lengthen; or narrower left/right arc (range but easier to
  get cornered). This is exactly the "spear combos start shorter and get longer" use case.
- **Ranged/melee balance philosophy (unresolved):** ranged should probably deal **less damage**
  and/or have **weapon-specific range** (bow > blowgun > thrown star) so it can't hit
  off-screen — he felt he was hitting enemies off-screen.

### 3e. Aiming / controls
- **Facing-locked shooting feels bad** for ranged. Two paths discussed: **twin-stick** (the real
  fix, later) and **mouse-aim** as an interim. He pitched the **Binding of Isaac** scheme —
  WASD to move, arrow keys to shoot (still cardinal-ish). Worth prototyping as the keyboard
  ranged control.
- **Hold-to-fire on the bow** — he *likes* being able to hold the attack for continuous bow
  fire. Keep it.

### 3f. Maze chests behind secrets (not on the path)
Beyond the C1 collision fix: chests landing mid-walkway feel wrong even when passable. Both of
us like **gating the chest behind exploration/a secret** rather than just placing it deep.
Concrete idea we both liked: a **pushable fake wall** (Zelda-1 "burn the bush," but less
obscure) — reveal a chest by shoving a block. Needs maze-generation geometry changes to leave
room for it. Longer-term: class-gated secrets (a gap only some classes cross, something to burn
down) as a *main-room* chest mechanic, separate from mazes.

### 3g. Mage as a spellcaster (not just a bolt hose)
Should the mage get **discrete spells** instead of one staff bolt? Leaning yes. Model Miguel
liked: **pick up spells like weapons, swap with Q/E**, favor **AOE** over single-target.
Keybinding is easy on keyboard (1–9), unclear on controller — that's the blocker. Related: he
felt **mage bolts should pierce** and **stars shouldn't**; my intuition is the reverse (a bolt
stops, a spinning star ricochets). Note the boomerang already passes through enemies. Unresolved
flavor call.

### 3h. Companion / pet (parked)
He floated a tamed-monster companion (follows you, attacks enemies, has an HP bar, dies when
depleted — no timer). I think it's complicated. Possible lightweight version: a **throwable
one-use trinket/potion** that charms an enemy to follow you for a while — I already have aggro
built, so redirecting a creature's target is "a knob to turn." The aggro-priority question
(player vs. pet) is the unsolved part. Also raised as a possible **Druid** sub-class of Mage —
but I don't want class branches yet. **Parked.**

## 4. Decisions still open

- **D-cap** — inventory weapon cap: **1 or 2?**
- **D-floors** — total floor count and floor size. Currently **10** (arbitrary; not enough
  bosses for it). Considering **3–4 large floors** (I have bosses for that). Target per-floor
  time from Miguel: **full exploration ≤ 8–10 min**; wants to time a full clear vs. a speed
  run. Floors should get **bigger**.
- **D-heal** — full-heal-every-floor vs. partial vs. heal stations in shops. Lean: add other
  heal sources, then remove the automatic full heal.
- **D-C1** — maze chest *placement*: gate behind a secret (pushable wall), and/or keep off the
  main walkway even though it's now non-solid.
- **D-C4** — make the trap tile read as a **hazard**, not a shortcut (it currently rewards
  stepping on it with a heal + skip).
- **D-C6** — throwing star: **nerf damage** (my lean) vs. **remove/cap pierce** (his lean).
- **D-buffs** — shrink buff magnitudes (favored) vs. reduce frequency. Keep buff-every-room.
- **D-armor** — adopt Armor/Helmet/Necklace/Ring slots as a gold sink + weapon-flood diluter;
  Miguel is drafting the effect list.
- **D-weaponbuff-scope** — which effects are player-permanent vs. weapon-bound vs. timed.
- **D-mage** — discrete swappable spells (Q/E, AOE) vs. staff bolt; controller keybinding is
  the blocker.
- **D-ranged-balance** — ranged damage/range tuning and whether to add weapon-specific range.

## 6. What's validated

- **Co-op is still the headline goal** and hasn't been re-tested since the last session — this
  was solo. Next test should be multiplayer.
- **The core loop is fun even under heavy bug load** — his verdict, unprompted. Buff-stacking is
  the current dopamine hit.
- **Randomized floor-1 layout + moved spawn** landed well ("the map changed layout and my spawn
  point! Neat!").
- **Reward-every-room** (Hades-style room value) is liked so far; watch for it getting
  "out of hand" — the mitigation is tuning rewards down or scaling enemies up, not removing it.
- **The new flagstone background** got an immediate positive first reaction.
- He's **shared the build with a friend** for an additional outside test — expect more feedback.
